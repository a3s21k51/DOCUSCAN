Privacy Policy for DocuScan

Application: DocuScan (DocScan) • Package: com.aistudio.docuscan.app • Last Updated: September 14, 2026

🔒 Core Privacy Commitment

DocuScan is built with a privacy-first, offline-first architecture. Your documents, photos, scanned pages, and extracted text are stored and processed strictly on your local Android device. We do not operate remote servers, do not require user accounts, and never track or sell your data.

1. Information We Collect and Process

DocuScan does not collect or transmit personal information to third parties:

  • No User Account: You do not need to register, provide your email, phone number, or name to use any feature of the app.
  • No Document Uploads: Photos taken through the scanner, imported PDFs, and OCR text transcripts are stored exclusively in your device's internal storage sandbox.
  • No Analytics or Ad Trackers: DocuScan does not contain advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or user profiling tools.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect your Android Advertising ID (AAID), IMEI, MAC address, or IP address.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

DocuScan requests only the minimal permissions required to deliver document scanning and file management:

Permission Usage & Purpose Data Handling Policy
CAMERA
android.permission.CAMERA		 Used to take photos of documents, receipts, business cards, and IDs for digital scanning. Camera frames are processed in-memory in real time on the device. Video or photos are never streamed or uploaded to the cloud.
INTERNET
android.permission.INTERNET		 Optional system communication (e.g. system share intents or opening documentation). No scanned content or personal data is transmitted over the internet by the app.
CAMERA FEATURE
android.hardware.camera		 Declared as optional hardware requirement. Allows the app to install on devices that import existing images from gallery without camera requirements.

3. On-Device OCR & Document Intelligence

All Optical Character Recognition (OCR), document edge detection, color enhancement filters, digital signatures, and PDF generation algorithms run entirely on your smartphone or tablet using local computing resources. None of your extracted text or scan images are transmitted to external cloud APIs.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your documents or data to any third party. The only time your data leaves the app is when you explicitly choose to share a document (such as via email, messaging, or cloud backup) through the standard Android system share dialog (Intent.ACTION_SEND).

5. User Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because all data is stored on your device, you have complete ownership and control:

  • Manual Document Deletion: You can delete any individual document, scanned page, or signature directly within the app at any time.
  • One-Tap Storage Wipe: Under the Menu > Storage screen, you can tap "Wipe All Stored Scans" to permanently remove all scans, cached files, and OCR records.
  • Application Uninstall: Uninstalling the application completely deletes all files stored in the app's private application directory.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

DocuScan does not knowingly collect or solicit information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, our application is safe for users of all ages and fully compliant with COPPA and GDPR regulations.

7. Google Play Console Data Safety Summary

For Google Play Console declarations:

  • Data Collection: "Does your app collect or share any user data?" → NO.
  • Data Security: All user documents stay strictly on the user's device.
  • Data Deletion: Users have the ability to delete all stored scans locally at any time.
